About Nairobi Radio TV

Our Mission

To discover, showcase, and elevate Kenya’s emerging musical talents across all genres, connecting raw passion with global recognition and sustainable success.

Our Vision

To be Africa’s premier youth-led media platform and the ultimate global engine for Kenyan music.

Our Philosophy

Talent is everywhere, but opportunity is not. We exist to bridge that gap for Kenya’s young artists—redefining the local music blueprint and driving our cultural heartbeat onto the world stage.
